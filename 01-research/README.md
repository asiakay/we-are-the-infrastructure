# Research

Research is R&D for the operating system, not homework that must be completed before action.

Organize this layer into:

- `sources/` — books, papers, public records, datasets, interviews, archives, and other source references.
- `reading-notes/` — notes tied closely to individual sources.
- `evidence/` — evidence packages supporting or challenging claims used by the framework and cases.
- `synthesis/` — concepts that have been compared across sources and translated into implications for the reasoning kernel, methods, curriculum, or software.

A useful research concept should eventually answer: **Does this improve our ability to observe, verify, map, diagnose, decide, build, act, or evaluate?**
