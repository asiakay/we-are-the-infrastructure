# Design Principles

These principles constrain how WE ARE THE INFRASTRUCTURE is researched, taught, implemented, and encoded in software.

## 1. Capability over consumption
The measure of success is not how much content someone encounters. It is what they can reliably understand, decide, build, or do afterward.

## 2. Agency over dependency
Tools, experts, educators, and software should increase the user's capacity to act without them rather than manufacture permanent dependence.

## 3. Evidence before certainty
Distinguish observations, claims, interpretations, hypotheses, and conclusions. Preserve uncertainty when the evidence does not justify certainty.

## 4. Lived knowledge is data, not infallibility
Experience can reveal conditions that formal systems overlook. It deserves investigation and articulation without being exempt from verification or competing explanation.

## 5. Strategy happens in time
A strategic choice is not merely what to do. It is what to change, by when, in what sequence, under what conditions, and with what consequences for future possibility.

## 6. Build before scale
Do not scale an unproven method. Establish that a practice creates capability in real conditions before automating or expanding it.

## 7. Preserve institutional memory
Actions should leave behind usable evidence, lessons, methods, relationships, and artifacts so each generation does not have to rediscover the same knowledge.

## 8. Design for transmission
Tacit expertise should be made teachable without pretending every form of knowledge can be reduced to a checklist.

## 9. Design for succession
Critical capabilities should not depend indefinitely on a single charismatic, knowledgeable, connected, or technically skilled person.

## 10. Technology is an implementation layer
The application serves the method. The method does not exist to justify the application.

## 11. Prefer reversible learning
Under uncertainty, favor bounded interventions that create evidence, preserve options, and permit revision over irreversible commitments based on weak assumptions.

## 12. Ask the second-order question
Every meaningful change should trigger:

> **What does that change make possible?**

The framework seeks compounding capability rather than isolated wins.
