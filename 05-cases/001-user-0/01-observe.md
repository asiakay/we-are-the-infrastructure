# 01 — Observe

## Purpose

Describe the present condition before explaining it.

This stage collects what is observable about User 0's current assets, flows, obligations, constraints, activities, and outcomes. It deliberately separates **observation** from **interpretation** and **diagnosis**.

At this stage, statements such as "the problem is distribution," "I need a job," "I need more visibility," or "I have too many projects" are hypotheses or interpretations—not yet findings.

## Observation rule

For each entry, distinguish where possible among:

- **Observed fact** — directly known or documented.
- **Reported experience** — User 0's account of what has occurred.
- **Estimate** — approximate quantity or condition.
- **Interpretation** — a proposed meaning that should be carried forward for verification rather than treated as fact.
- **Unknown** — information that appears relevant but is not yet known.

Do not force false precision. An explicitly recorded unknown is better than an invented number.

# G. Capacity snapshot

Capacity is a constraint, not an assumption.

The case should eventually estimate the amount and type of capacity actually available across major areas of life and work. This is not merely a calendar exercise: different activities may require different kinds of cognitive, emotional, physical, relational, or logistical capacity.

Questions to observe later include:

- What capacity is reliably available?
- What capacity is volatile?
- What consumes capacity unexpectedly?
- Which activities restore capacity?
- Which forms of work compete for the same kind of capacity?
- Where is maintenance overhead being ignored?

# Exit criteria for Observe

Do not advance merely because every heading contains text.

Observation is sufficiently developed when:

- the major assets and active flows are visible;
- capacity-consuming obligations are represented;
- important constraints are recorded without prematurely classifying them as causes;
- current activities can be distinguished from desired activities;
- observable outcomes are separated from effort;
- consequential unknowns have been surfaced; and
- we can state the present condition without needing a causal explanation to make the description coherent.

Only then move to **02 — Verify**.
